Roulette Rules & Payouts

How to Play

Roulette is a classic game of chance. Select a chip value and place it on any field or intersection on the betting board to lock in your prediction. When you click SPIN, the wheel spins and a ball is rolled. You win if the ball lands in a pocket that matches your bet coordinates.

Betting Options

  • Straight Up: Place directly on a number (including 0).
  • Split: Place on the line dividing two numbers.
  • Street: Place on the bottom line of a column of 3 numbers.
  • Corner: Place on the intersection of four numbers.
  • Line: Place on the intersection of six numbers (two adjacent columns).
  • Dozens / Columns: Bet on groups of 12 numbers.
  • Even/Odd, Red/Black, High/Low: Even money (1:1) outside bets.
